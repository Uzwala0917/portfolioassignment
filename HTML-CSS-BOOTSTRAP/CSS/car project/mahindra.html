    The Mahindra Scorpio is a popular mid-size body-on-frame SUV manufactured by the Indian automaker Mahindra & Mahindra since 2002. The traditional, rugged workhorse model known for its high stance, muscular road presence, and utilitarian appeal.

    OVERVIEW

    The Big Daddy of SUVs

    One grip on the steering-wheel is all it takes to unleash the commanding presence, thrilling capability and dominance of the Scorpio-N. You'll know it the moment you drive it.

    CAR FEATURES

    Infotainment & Screens

    Features a large 12.3-inch touchscreen infotainment display powered by the AdrenoX cockpit system alongside a 10.25-inch fully digital instrument cluster that shows adventure data like pitch, roll, and tire direction.

    Cabin Comfort

    Equipped with a two-pane panoramic sunroof, dual-zone automatic climate control, front ventilated seats, a 6-way power-adjustable driver’s seat, and a premium 12-speaker Sony sound system.

    Safety Suite

    Outfitted with Level 2 ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems), 6 airbags, Electronic Stability Control (ESC), driver drowsiness detection, and all-wheel disc brakes.

    Utility

    Offers 65W fast USB-C charging ports, a wireless phone charging pad, over 70 connected car features, and an auto-dimming rearview mirror.
