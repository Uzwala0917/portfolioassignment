Yoshito Usui (臼井儀人) (21 April 1958 ~ 11 September 2009) was a manga artist best-known for the manga Crayon Shin-chan. Born in Shizuoka Prefecture, Japan, he was educated in the city of Kasukabe in Saitama Prefecture, where he resided with his family before his death.
